My Mission

To provide developers with a featherlight, performance-driven toolkit to build stunning, animated web experiences without the bloat of traditional frameworks.

The Spark Behind STRIKE

STRIKE was born out of a simple frustration: modern web development is often weighed down by heavy JS libraries and complex build tools just to create simple animations. I wanted to go back to basics and prove the power of pure CSS.

This project is a testament to what can be achieved with clean HTML and modern CSS, focusing on creativity and raw performance.
